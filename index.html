Description

The Pinball Roguelike! PINBOMB traps you in a room with a pinball machine hungry for higher and higher scores. Buy new parts to upgrade it, build wild synergies to feed the machine, and send your score through the roof. Oh, and there's a bomb strapped to it, so score big or go boom!

Features

  • 200+ unique and handmade pinball machine parts, custom balls, active abilities, enchantments, and customizations.
  • In-depth run structure containing branching paths with different bosses, optional content, and extra challenges.
  • Unlock new starting boards with unique layouts and parts.
  • Optional escalating difficulty levels for players who want to put their skills to the test.
  • Old-school visuals, flashing lights, and juicy feedback that inject dopamine straight into your eyeballs.
