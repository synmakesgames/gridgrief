Description

The Pitch (Short)

A roguelite plinko-style deckbuilder about escaping the space between heaven & hell. Forge powerful synergies, trigger chaotic screen filling effects and rack up huge scores to secure your resurrection.

The Game (Long)

Grid Grief is a roguelite deckbuilder where players navigate a haunting afterlife by strategically placing soulstones on an ever evolving grid. Every placement is a gamble between progression and permanent loss, forcing you to weigh immediate rewards against long term survival.

As your run goes on, you will uncover unique stones, perks and secrets that trigger explosive combos. By collecting and placing pins, you can create "game-breaking" synergies that snowball a standard run into a rule bending escape attempt.

Your only escape path is to build a grid capable of shattering a god.

Grid Grief combines the plinko style of Peggle with roguelike mechanics, putting a unique pin-building twist on the genre inspired by games like Clover Pit and Nubby's Number Factory.

At its heart, Grid Grief is a dark tale of calculation and escape, where strategic placement determines whether you transcend to glory or remain lost in grief.

Core Features

  • Rogue-lite Strategy: A high-stakes deckbuilder where every round could be your last.
  • 100+ Unique Effects: Endless variety in how you influence the outcome of your run.
  • Game Breaking Synergies: Discover perks and stones combinations that allow you to bend the rules and break the game.
  • Meta Progression: Unlockable stronger stones to aid your escape.
  • Plinky Plonking! Addictive mechanics centered around forging a god destroying grid.
